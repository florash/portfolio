Cloudy Clothing

A soft, minimal fashion ecommerce concept created to combine clean brand presentation with a simple and intuitive shopping journey.

Open Video Walkthrough More Projects

Overview

Project Goal

Cloudy Clothing was developed as a fashion ecommerce concept focused on creating a calmer online shopping experience. Instead of a busy storefront, the goal was to make product discovery feel clean, soft, and easy to move through while still supporting the practical parts of browsing and buying.

My Role

What I Worked On

  • Handled the visual styling and page presentation across the storefront.
  • Worked on WordPress theme customisation and WooCommerce setup.
  • Shaped product page presentation along with cart and checkout flow.
  • Adjusted the responsive layout to support a smoother mobile shopping experience.

Tools

WooCommerce Responsive Storefront Product Styling Checkout Flow

Outcome

Result

The finished concept creates a more cohesive shopping journey, with clearer product presentation and a softer brand feel throughout the store. It demonstrates my ability to connect visual direction with practical ecommerce structure, including product browsing, purchase flow, and responsive behaviour.
