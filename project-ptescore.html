Product Case Study · EdTech · 2026

PTEscore.

A bilingual PTE Academic practice product that brings exam-style tasks, AI-assisted scoring, audio practice and progress visibility into one focused learning experience.

RoleProduct designer & developer
PlatformsWeb · iOS · iPad
FocusPractice · Scoring · Progress
StatusIndependent product

The challenge

One exam, many different practice behaviours.

PTE preparation spans speaking, writing, reading and listening, each with different interaction patterns, timing and feedback needs. The product needed to keep this complexity understandable while still feeling fast enough for daily practice.

I designed the experience around a repeatable loop: choose a task, complete it with the right input tools, review feedback, then use progress signals to decide what to practise next.

Core product decisions

A system designed around focused improvement.

01 · Practice

Exam-style task flows

Question-specific layouts support recording, listening, writing, selection and timed completion without forcing every task into the same interface.

02 · Feedback

Readable score detail

Results are organised to make scoring useful for the next attempt, rather than presenting a number without learning context.

03 · Continuity

Progress over time

Practice history and performance signals help learners return with a clearer sense of where to focus.

Learning flow

From question discovery to the next best action.

01Choose

Browse by skill and question type.

02Practise

Complete the task in an exam-aware interface.

03Review

Read scores, feedback and answer support.

04Improve

Return to weak areas and track change.

My contribution

Designed and built as one connected product.

I worked across product structure, interface design, responsive implementation, question data, audio workflows, scoring access, bilingual content and native-device considerations.

The key learning was that an education product is not only a collection of questions. Its value comes from connecting content, feedback and repetition in a way that keeps the learner oriented.
